🙏 vs 🙌 vs 👏: What Do These Hand Emojis Mean?

By EmojiSpot · About 6 minutes

Not sure whether to send 🙏, 🙌 or 👏? All three can fit a positive message, but they put the emphasis in different places. 🙏 often says “thank you” or “please,” 🙌 celebrates the moment, and 👏 gives someone a round of applause.

If a friend helps you finish a project, you might thank them with 🙏. When the project is finally done, 🙌 fits the celebration. And if they did a brilliant job? That’s a good moment for 👏.

Here’s a closer look at these hand emoji meanings—and how to choose between them without overthinking your next text.

🙏, 🙌 and 👏 meanings at a glance

Hand emoji names, meanings and example messages
Emoji Name Common meaning Example message
🙏 Folded hands Thanks, a polite request, prayer or respect “Thanks for staying to help 🙏”
🙌 Raising hands Celebration, joy or enthusiastic praise “We finally finished it! 🙌”
👏 Clapping hands Applause, congratulations or recognition “You did a brilliant job 👏”

These are starting points, not strict rules. Unicode’s emoji names and keywords include associations such as thanks and prayer for 🙏, celebration and praise for 🙌, and applause and congratulations for 👏. Your words help the recipient understand which meaning you intend.

🙏 Folded hands emoji meaning: thanks, please or prayer

The folded hands emoji shows two palms pressed together. It’s useful when you want to express gratitude, ask sincerely or mention prayer. It can also accompany a respectful greeting, such as namaste. Emojipedia’s folded hands entry describes these overlapping uses.

You don’t have to be talking about religion to use it:

  • For thanks: “You saved me so much time—thank you 🙏”
  • For a request: “Could you send me the address, please? 🙏”
  • For prayer: “Praying for a safe journey 🙏”

Notice how the sentence does most of the explaining. Without those words, a single 🙏 could be a thank-you, a request or something more personal. If you’ve asked someone a question, don’t assume their 🙏 means “yes.”

Is 🙏 a high five?

Some people use or interpret it that way. However, its name is folded hands, and thanks, requests and prayer are common associations. If you mean a high five, simply write “High five!” rather than leaving the other person to guess.

🙌 Raising hands emoji meaning: celebration and praise

Think of 🙌 as the moment you throw your hands up and say, “Yes! We did it!” The raising hands emoji is closely associated with celebrating success or happy news, as described in Emojipedia’s raising hands entry.

It fits messages like:

  • “We reached our goal! 🙌”
  • “You got the tickets! 🙌”
  • “The missing file is recovered 🙌”

You can celebrate your own good news, someone else’s achievement or a shared win. You don’t need to have been involved to join the cheer.

You may also hear 🙌 called “praise hands.” Praise can be celebratory or, in a religious conversation, worship-related. So it’s better not to draw a hard line that says 🙏 is religious and 🙌 never is. The conversation gives you the clue.

👏 Clapping hands emoji meaning: applause and “well done”

The clapping hands emoji puts the focus on recognition. You’re not just pleased that something happened—you’re applauding it.

Try it when someone deserves a little credit:

  • “You sang beautifully 👏”
  • “That took real patience—well done 👏”
  • “Congratulations on finishing your course 👏”

A repeated 👏👏👏 can suggest a round of applause, matching Emojipedia’s description of clapping hands.

Of course, applause can be sarcastic too. “Great, you deleted the file 👏” could sound mocking rather than supportive. If you want your praise to feel sincere, saying what you appreciate helps: “Your explanation made that so much easier 👏.”

The same message, three different replies

Imagine a friend tells you:

“I stayed late and finished our presentation.”

Any of the three emojis could work. What changes is the part you want to emphasise:

Three replies to the same message
Your reply What it highlights
“Thank you for doing that 🙏” Appreciation for their help
“It’s ready—we did it! 🙌” Shared celebration or relief
“You did a great job 👏” Recognition of their effort

That’s the practical difference between folded hands, raising hands and clapping hands. You’re choosing a tone—not trying to pass an emoji test.

Which hand emoji should you use?

Ask yourself what you want the message to do:

  • Thank someone or ask for a favour? Start with 🙏.
  • Celebrate good news? Try 🙌.
  • Recognise someone’s work or achievement? Try 👏.

There’s room for overlap. “Thanks for a fantastic performance 🙏👏” can express both gratitude and applause. These are suggested uses, not combinations with an official coded meaning.

Can I use 🙌 and 👏 together?

Yes. “You passed! 🙌👏” combines celebration with applause. If that feels too enthusiastic for your usual style, one emoji and a few words work just as well.

Which one works best in a work message?

Match the purpose of the message and the communication style of your workplace. “Thanks for checking 🙏,” “Launch complete 🙌,” and “Great work on the report 👏” each make their intention clear.

For formal or sensitive conversations, don’t leave the important part to an emoji. Write the thank-you, congratulations or request in words first.

Does the sender’s age or gender change the meaning?

Neither gives you a dependable rule for decoding a message. Look at the conversation, your relationship and the person’s usual style. If their meaning matters and you’re unsure, asking is more useful than guessing.

A simple way to remember

🙏 Appreciate or ask. 🙌 Celebrate. 👏 Applaud.

Use that as a starting point, then add the words you’d actually say to the person. A thoughtful sentence does more for your tone than finding the supposedly perfect emoji.
