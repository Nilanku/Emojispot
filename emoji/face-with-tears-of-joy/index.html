face with tears of joy

Copy 😂 or explore its meaning, examples and related emojis below.

😂

What does 😂 mean?

😂 commonly means laughing hard or finding something very funny.

It can react to a joke, a funny mishap or a playful story. Some communities also use 😭 or 💀 for laughter, but there is no universal age-based rule for which emoji to choose.

Example message

I watched that clip three times 😂

Common uses

  • Reacting to a joke or a friend's misfortune in a group chat.
  • Laughing at yourself after a small disaster, like sending the wrong file to a client.
  • Taking the edge off a complaint so it reads as teasing rather than serious.
  • Replying to a meme with no words at all.
  • Punctuating a caption that ends in a punchline.

Context and possible misunderstandings

Laughter can feel dismissive under serious news. Sarcasm depends on the surrounding message; repeated 😂 does not reliably tell you what someone really feels.

A common question

How is 😂 different from 🤣?

Both convey laughter. 🤣 depicts exaggerated rolling-on-the-floor laughter, while 😂 depicts a laughing face with tears. People may use either sincerely or sarcastically.

How to type 😂

Copy it using the button above, or open your device's emoji picker. Names and search support vary with keyboard language and software version.

  • Windows: press Windows + . (full stop) to open the emoji picker.
  • Mac: Control + Command + Space usually opens the character viewer.
  • iPhone or iPad: use the emoji key, or switch keyboards with the globe key. Enable the emoji keyboard in Settings if needed.
  • Android: open your keyboard's emoji panel. In Gboard, use the emoji key and search if available.

Meanings are context-dependent descriptions of common usage, not rules about what any particular sender intends.
