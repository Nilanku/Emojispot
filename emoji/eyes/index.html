What does 👀 mean?

👀 often expresses attention, curiosity, anticipation, or noticing something interesting.

It can suggest “I'm watching” or “I noticed that.” Under a photo it may show appreciation; during a story it may invite the person to continue. It can also refer literally to eyes or looking.

Example message

You said there was news—tell me more 👀

Common uses

  • Signalling you have seen something without replying to it.
  • Asking for gossip to continue.
  • Showing interest in someone's appearance.
  • Pointing out that something suspicious just happened.
  • Reacting to a bold or unexpected statement.

Context and possible misunderstandings

It does not prove attraction or suspicion. A short comment such as “Looking forward to it” makes your intention clearer.

A common question

Is 👀 the same as 👁️?

👀 shows two eyes and often conveys a reaction. 👁️ shows a single eye and can refer to sight or observation. Both can be used literally or symbolically.
