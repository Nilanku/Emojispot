EmojiSpot - QA fix (23 October 2026)
===================================

WHAT THIS FIXES
---------------
1. The emoji detail card opens on a phone (press and hold) and STAYS open.
   Before, it appeared and vanished the instant you lifted your finger.
2. The same card now opens on a computer with a RIGHT-CLICK on any emoji.
   Before, there was no way to reach it with a mouse at all.
3. No more sideways scroll on phones (it happened on the skin-tone pages,
   which were 14 pixels wider than the screen).
4. Every translated page was one release behind: they showed 1,906 emoji
   instead of 1,923, so the 17 new emoji were missing from the Hindi,
   Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, French, German and Indonesian categories,
   and /hi/emoji/pickle/ + /es/emoji/pickle/ were "not found".
   Now every translated category lists all 1,923 emoji, the counts are
   correct, and all 17 new emoji have Hindi and Spanish pages of their own.
5. Slightly bigger tap targets: skin-tone swatches and the small "x"
   inside the search box.

HOW TO UPLOAD (about 3 minutes)
-------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com/Nilanku/Emojispot
2. Click the green "Code" button, then "Upload files".
3. Unzip this file on your computer. Drag EVERYTHING that is inside it
   (the folders assets, hi, es, ar, pt, fr, de, id plus the two files
   sitemap.xml and sw.js) onto the GitHub upload page.
   Upload the folders themselves - not the zip file.
   If GitHub complains about too many files at once, upload one folder
   at a time: assets, then hi, then es, then the rest.
4. Scroll down, write a short message such as
   "QA fixes: detail card, phone width, translated pages" and click
   "Commit changes".
5. Cloudflare rebuilds on its own in about a minute.

AFTER IT IS LIVE - 5 QUICK CHECKS
---------------------------------
1. On your phone press and hold any emoji for half a second
   -> the detail card slides up and stays.
2. On a laptop right-click any emoji -> the same card opens.
3. Open /tone/light/ on your phone and drag left/right
   -> the page must not wobble sideways.
4. Open /hi/emoji/pickle/ -> a real page, not "not found".
5. Open /hi/smileys-emotion/ -> 172 emoji, same as English.
